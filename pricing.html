Questions

Do you offer refunds?

No. Once a job is delivered it can't be refunded, which is why we agree the scope clearly before I start.

What should I put in my message?

What you need built, your budget and your deadline. Messages that only say "are you free?" don't get a reply.

How long does a job take?

Modules usually take a day or two and full systems a few days. Larger builds get a timeline before work starts.

Do you take long-term work?

Yes. I take on retainers for live games, ideally with 1,000+ concurrent players. Send me your game and what you're planning.
